## NeuCodec unfuse (asked late session)

Neu already has two towers (W2vBERT frozen + acoustic CNN). Concat → one 16-bit
FSQ @ 50 Hz → Vocos is a **training choice**. Keeping them separate is the
right diagnosis for fused-AR stretch.

Cannot keep stock Vocos or Air AR. Vocos was trained on **one** id per 20 ms.
Two streams = new decoder + new LM interface = a new stack, not a Neu flag.

| after unfuse | decoder | LM | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2× FSQ + retrained Vocos | new Vocos | 2 AR or delay-pattern | nobody ships |
| 2× FSQ + DiT | DSA | 1 interleaved or zs-LM | HF recon not usable here |
| **FSQ_s + flow** | CosyVoice / F5 | **1** LM on zs; speaker from ref | what actually works |
| FSQ_s + RVQ_a | Dual / Flexi | delay-pattern | works; not 2 FSQ |

Optimal for clone-without-fused-stretch: LM **stops writing acoustics**.
Keep Neu’s **50 Hz** and FSQ on **content only**. Prosody/timbre = flow (or
RVQ **behind** the LM) from a short ref. Two FSQ + two LMs is DSA’s bet;
encoder exists, decoder we could not use.

## Flow-stack probe: where should the reference enter? (2026-09-29 evening, DONE, unblinded)

Handoff section. Read this plus [v7-v8-dets.md](v7-v8-dets.md) (why: v8 copies and amplifies the reference's
timing into dead air / slow speech on bad or everyday refs) to pick up the thread.

### Why we ran it

The user wanted to keep NeuCodec's two towers (w2v-BERT semantic + acoustic CNN) separate instead of fusing them
into one FSQ, and asked whether a CosyVoice-2/3-style design (one AR over content tokens, a separate flow model for
the acoustics) would work. Before building anything, we tested whether stacks that already have that shape still
copy a bad reference's timing. Research on the web (same day) found no public two-FSQ codec with a working decoder
other than DSA (which failed recon above). The working pattern is content tokens + flow. See the lit list below.

Key design question: the ref can enter (1) as raw codes inside the LM prompt (Air, CosyVoice zero-shot),
(2) only in the flow model (CosyVoice cross-lingual mode: the frontend deletes prompt text + prompt speech tokens
from the LM), or (3) as a pooled fixed-length summary into the LM (IndexTTS2: conformer + perceiver).

### What it tells us

1. **The dead air is caused by our fine-tune, not by the architecture.** CosyVoice3 with ref tokens in the LM (ICL, like
   Air) copies the slow pace but makes no ≥2 s holes. Stock Air is nearly clean as well. Only v8 A breaks.
2. **Keeping the ref out of the LM stops pace copying but loses the voice.** `cx` has 0 gaps and WER 0.005, but it speaks at a
   fixed pace, and its SIM on the user's voice falls to 0.56-0.69.
3. **A pooled ref gives the best of both.** IndexTTS2 keeps the voice (SIM 0.93-0.97) and does not copy hesitation. This
   refines the LOCK above: the ref should reach the AR model as a **fixed-length pooled summary**, not as raw codes and not
   only through the flow model.
4. **The user's split-codec idea already exists in working form.** IndexTTS2's content tokens come from **w2v-BERT 2.0**, the
   same tower as NeuCodec's semantic half, and its acoustics come from a flow model. That is effectively "unfused Neu".

### Which codec / model suits us

| stack | content tokens (what the AR predicts) | acoustics | ref entry | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IndexTTS2** | w2v-BERT 2.0 → VQ (MaskGCT semantic codec, 8192) | flow-matching S2M → BigVGAN v2, 22 kHz | pooled (perceiver) | **Reference design.** Best by ear, voice and robustness. zh/en only. |
| CosyVoice3 | MinMo encoder + FSQ, 25 Hz, 6561 codes (ASR, emotion, LID and event supervised) | DiT flow → HiFT, 24 kHz | in the LM or flow-only | Solid. Choose between pace robustness and voice. No Hindi. |
| NeuTTS-Air stock | w2v-BERT + acoustic CNN fused into one FSQ, 50 Hz | inside the LM tokens → Vocos | raw codes in the LM | OK as stock; loses the user's voice and accent. |
| v8 A (our Air FT) | same | same | same | Copies and amplifies bad refs. Not shippable on everyday refs. |

### Caveats

- English only. The Hindi route still needs our own content tokenizer (w2v-BERT is multilingual) and training.
- Scale confound: the stock stacks trained on 100k+ h, versus our fine-tune data.
- The ear pages use seed 7 on 2 lines per ref; the metrics use 3 seeds. n is enough for a big effect, not a small one.
- Whisper can hallucinate words over noise, which would hide a gap. `cx` fillers count as WER insertions, but its WER is still 0.005.
