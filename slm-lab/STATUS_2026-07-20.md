## main.md critique — assessed 2026-07-20, largely refuted
External teardown claimed "residual collapse" (query norm ~0 by iter 3, picks 3-8 = noise).
**Measured false**: residual keeps 96.7% of norm; cos_orig flat ~.20 through pick 16; and if
picks 3-8 were noise OMP would tie uniform, which it does not (p=.00059).
Its prescribed fix (linear-space feature DPP) **was already run and ties OMP** — and, as the
doc itself later concedes, that fix IS LDDR Algorithm 1 (zero novelty).
Right conclusion (signal dead past pick ~5), wrong mechanism (cos_resid collapse, not norm
collapse). Mechanism matters: it points at the scorer/query, not the geometry.

**What the critique gets right and we cannot answer yet:**
- Bag-of-frames / **shuffle invariance** — our selector is permutation-invariant; shuffle the
  video, identical 8 picks. No temporal term anywhere. Never tested.
- Novelty framing: our claim is empirical/systems, not mathematical.

LDDR distinction confirmed: LDDR orthogonalizes **frames against selected frames** in a
relevance-warped space (Phi = diag(r)F), query static as a magnitude scaler. We orthogonalize
**query against picked frames**. Different objects. Not a copy.
